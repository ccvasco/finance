## Listing Structure Deep Dive

### 1. **A-Shares (000333.SZ)**

| Attribute | Details |
|-----------|---------|
| **Exchange** | Shenzhen Stock Exchange (SZSE) |
| **Ticker Format** | 6 digits + `.SZ` |
| **Currency** | Renminbi (CNY) |
| **Accessibility** | Via Shanghai-Hong Kong Stock Connect on IBKR |
| **Foreign Ownership Limit** | 30% ceiling (can suspend buy orders) |
| **Typical Trader** | Domestic Chinese investors |

**Pros:**
- High domestic liquidity and trading volume
- Familiar trading hours for China-based investors
- Direct access to domestic market sentiment

**Cons:**
- **Priced at 9-10% premium** vs. H-shares for identical equity
- Subject to 30% foreign investor cap
- Stock Connect eligibility required on IBKR
- CNY currency conversion needed for non-China holders

---

### 2. **H-Shares (0300.HK)**

| Attribute | Details |
|-----------|---------|
| **Exchange** | Hong Kong Stock Exchange (HKEX) |
| **Ticker Format** | 5 digits + `.HK` |
| **Currency** | Hong Kong Dollar (HKD) |
| **Accessibility** | Direct HK market access on IBKR |
| **Foreign Ownership Limit** | None |
| **Typical Trader** | International investors |

**Pros:**
- **Better valuation** (9-10% discount vs. A-shares)
- No 30% foreign ownership restriction
- Higher global liquidity for international buyers
- Standard IBKR access without Stock Connect setup
- HKD pegged to USD (lower FX volatility vs. CNY)

**Cons:**
- Hong Kong trading hours may not align with all timezones
- HKD currency exposure (though relatively stable)
- Slightly lower liquidity than Shenzhen for very large orders

---

### 3. **Frankfurt Listing (1520.F)**

| Attribute | Details |
|-----------|---------|
| **Exchange** | Deutsche Börse (FWB) |
| **Ticker Format** | 4 digits + `.F` |
| **Currency** | Euro (EUR) |
| **Accessibility** | Standard European market access on IBKR |
| **Foreign Ownership Limit** | None |
| **Typical Trader** | European investors |

**Pros:**
- Trades during EU business hours
- EUR denomination (ideal for Euro-zone investors)
- No special program access required
- Simplest IBKR setup for European traders

**Cons:**
- Lowest liquidity among the three listings
- Wider bid-ask spreads
- Lower trading volume = harder to execute large orders
- May experience temporary price dislocations

## IBKR Identification Guide

| Identifier | A-Share (000333.SZ) | H-Share (0300.HK) | Frankfurt (1520.F) |
|------------|---------------------|-------------------|-------------------|
| **Exchange Code** | XSHE or XSES | XHKG | XFRA |
| **Currency Display** | CNY | HKD | EUR |
| **Security Name** | Contains "Class A" | Contains "H Shares" | Often "Ordinary Shares" |
| **ISIN Prefix** | CNE1xxxxxx | HKG000xxxxx | DE000xxxxxx |
| **Required Setup** | Stock Connect enabled | Standard HK access | Standard EU access |

### Quick IBKR Search Tips:

1. **Symbol Lookup:** Enter full ticker with suffix (e.g., `000333.SZ`)
2. **Verify Exchange:** Check the exchange code in the order ticket
3. **Confirm Currency:** Ensure the settlement currency matches your expectations
4. **Check Settlement:** A-shares settle T+1 via Stock Connect; H-shares T+2 standard

## Valuation Comparison Framework

### When A-Shares Might Make Sense:

| Scenario | Rationale |
|----------|-----------|
| **China tax resident** | Currency alignment, simplified tax reporting |
| **Short-term trading** | Highest intraday liquidity |
| **Market momentum plays** | A-share market sentiment can drive faster moves |
| **Already have Stock Connect** | No additional setup friction |

### When H-Shares Might Make Sense:

| Scenario | Rationale |
|----------|-----------|
| **International investor** | Better valuation + no foreign ownership caps |
| **Long-term holder** | Save 9-10% on initial entry cost |
| **Large position builder** | Less likely to hit foreign ownership limits |
| **USD/HKD base currency** | Reduced FX complexity vs. CNY |
| **Morningstar analyst view** | Specifically recommended for overseas investors |

### When Frankfurt Might Make Sense:

| Scenario | Rationale |
|----------|-----------|
| **EU tax resident** | EUR denomination simplifies reporting |
| **European trading hours** | Can trade during local business hours |
| **IBKR Europe account** | No cross-border fee considerations |
| **Small position size** | Liquidity concerns minimal for modest investments |
